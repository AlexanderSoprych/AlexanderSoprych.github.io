Desert Tigers

A stylized isometric motif depicting DAK Tiger 1's roaming the dunes of Tunisia.

Left-click and drag to orbit.
Right-click and drag to pan.
Scroll up or down to zoom.

This work demonstrates my ability to render a stylized scene convincingly through the purposeful choice of mood-setting colors. The already boxy Tiger 1 lends itself perfectly to such a low-poly rendering. A lower-poly 3D scene demonstrating how just the proper use of color can evoke feeling irrespective of texture quality.

Instead of using actual materials, I allude to texture through the way in which the rough cubic geometry reacts with the scene's lighting as well as more simply by the solid block colors chosen for each shape. There is a heat conveyed through the scene that is hopefully evocative of the Tunisian desert.

It is surprising how well intricately detailed objects can be simplfied while still retaining their original recognizability. I dare not pretend to have wholly abstracted the Tiger 1, but instead in reducing its surfaces to a limited color palette, have produced a toy-like - or even game-like - result.
